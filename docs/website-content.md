## FAQ

**How does Optimile integrate with existing ERPs?**
Optimile is API-first and syncs purchase orders and invoices in real time with SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics and custom ERPs.

**Does Optimile replace my TMS?**
No. Optimile can be your primary TMS or layer on top of your existing one, improving routing, visibility, POD collection and settlement without a migration.

**Can it track market vehicles without GPS installed?**
Yes. Optimile uses SIM-based tracking with driver consent, and smartphone triangulation, to track ad-hoc market vehicles.

**How quickly can we go live?**
Most teams go live in phases within 4–8 weeks: connect the ERP, configure workflows per business unit, and onboard vendors and drivers.

**How does it reduce billing errors?**
Every invoice is validated against contract rules, route data, timestamps and POD before it's paid, so wrong rates, detention mismatches and missing documents are caught at source.

**Is it built for enterprise security?**
Yes. Encrypted pipelines, role-based access, audit logs on every change, and secure ERP integrations.

**Subhead:** Post a lane to your approved carriers and watch them bid it down, without leaking a competitor's price or losing the trail.

**What you get:**
- **Rank-only bidding.** Carriers see their own rank, never L1 or a competitor's price.
- **Anti-sniping.** Late bids extend the window, so the auction is decided on price, not timing.
- **Ranked fallback.** If L1 falls through, Optimile moves to L2, then L3, on rules you set.
- **An immutable trail.** Every bid is recorded and audited. Offline-negotiated rates can be logged without rewriting history.

**Proof point:** Competitive rates with a defensible record for every placement.
